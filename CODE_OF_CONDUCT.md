# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.

We welcome disagreement about ideas, implementations, benchmarks, and scientific claims. Critique the work, provide evidence, and avoid personal attacks or harassment.

Maintainers may remove contributions that are abusive, discriminatory, threatening, deliberately deceptive, or disruptive to constructive technical discussion.

For concerns, open a private report through the repository's available security/contact mechanism rather than posting sensitive information publicly.
